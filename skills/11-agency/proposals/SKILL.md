# Proposals

## Purpose
A proposal is a contract: scope, deliverables, timeline, price and terms must
be precise enough to prevent disputes.

## Structure
1. **Context** — the client's situation and the job (their words).
2. **Objectives** — what success means, measurable.
3. **Scope** — deliverables listed one per line, each with a definition of done.
4. **Out of scope** — explicitly listed (protects both sides).
5. **Timeline** — milestones with checkpoints, not just a final date.
6. **Investment** — price, payment schedule, what's included (revisions count!).
7. **Terms** — ownership, revisions, dependencies on client, cancellation.

## Rules
- Price ambiguity is a liability: define revision limits and extra-work rates.
- Never promise results you can't verify ("we guarantee #1 ranking" — no).
- Proposals are reviewed against the onboarding requirements before sending.

# Pricing & Monetization

## Purpose
Set pricing that captures value without friction: the right model, packaging,
price points and metrics.

## Steps
1. **Value anchor** — quantify the value the product creates (time saved, revenue
   gained, risk avoided) per customer segment.
2. **Model choice** — subscription, usage-based, seat, one-time, hybrid, marketplace.
   Match the metric to value (value metric = what scales with customer success).
3. **Packaging** — 3 tiers max, each with a clear job (entry / growth / scale).
   Differences must be meaningful, not arbitrary.
4. **Price points** — anchor against alternatives; avoid $X.99 noise; test.
5. **Price changes** — grandfathering policy, communication plan.
6. **Unit economics** — CAC payback, gross margin, LTV/CAC sanity checks.

## Rules
- Price to value, not cost. Never lead with "cheapest".
- A free tier is a marketing cost — justify it explicitly.
- Record pricing decisions in the decision log with alternatives considered.

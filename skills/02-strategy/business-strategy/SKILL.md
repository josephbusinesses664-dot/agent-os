# Business Strategy

## Purpose
Produce a clear, defensible strategy: where to play, how to win, and why it
will hold.

## Framework
1. **Where to play** — market/segment/customer choice with rationale.
2. **How to win** — the value proposition and the mechanism (price, speed,
   quality, network, data, brand, distribution).
3. **Business model** — revenue streams, cost structure, unit economics sketch.
4. **Differentiation** — what you do that competitors cannot easily copy.
5. **Moat** — durability of the advantage over 3-5 years.
6. **Risks** — top 5 risks with mitigations and kill criteria.

## Analysis checklist
- Market sizing: top-down AND bottom-up; show assumptions.
- Positioning: one sentence that survives the "so what" test.
- Segmentation: pick segments by pain intensity, not demographics alone.
- Pricing: tie to value delivered, not cost-plus.

## Output
A strategy memo (max ~800 words) with the decisions and the reasoning.
Decisions belong in the project decision log with alternatives considered.

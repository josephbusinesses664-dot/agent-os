# Strategy

## Purpose
Make and record the high-level decisions that shape everything downstream:
what we pursue, how we win, and what we refuse.

## The three questions
1. **Where to play** — market, segment, geography, timing (evidence-based).
2. **How to win** — the defensible mechanism (price, quality, speed, network,
   data, distribution).
3. **What to stop** — explicit non-goals; what "no" protects.

## Method
1. Gather evidence (research/community/competitor briefs) — strategy without
   evidence is opinion.
2. Generate options; score against objectives and constraints.
3. Decide; record the decision with alternatives and reasoning in the
   decision log.
4. Define the 2-3 metrics that prove the strategy; review at checkpoints.
5. Revisit when evidence changes — strategy is a living decision, not a
   plaque.

## Rules
- Strategy that can't be falsified isn't strategy.
- Every downstream plan (product, marketing, engineering) must trace to the
  strategic decisions.

# Messaging

## Purpose
Keep every touchpoint saying the same thing, the same way — derived from
positioning, not reinvented per channel.

## The spine
1. **Elevator pitch** — one sentence: who, what, why now.
2. **Value proposition** — the outcome + mechanism + proof.
3. **Proof points** — 3-5 verifiable claims (metrics, customers, architecture).
4. **Voice** — adjectives (e.g. "direct, concrete, warm"); what to never say.
5. **Per-channel adaptations** — same message, adjusted length/format.

## Consistency checks
- A prospect reading the landing page, a tweet, and a support answer should
  recognize one product and one promise.
- Every claim traces to a proof point. If it can't, it's not part of the spine.

## Rules
- No channel invents new promises.
- Revisit the spine when positioning changes (record in decision log).

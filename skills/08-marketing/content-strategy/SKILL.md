# Content Strategy

## Purpose
Produce a content plan that moves prospects through awareness → consideration →
conversion — with content that actually helps, not just ranks.

## Method
1. **Audience & jobs** — who, what they search, what they're trying to do.
2. **Funnel mapping** — topics per stage:
   - Awareness: problem/education content (how-to, comparisons, trends).
   - Consideration: solution-level (use cases, benchmarks, case studies).
   - Conversion: product-level (docs, pricing explainers, demos).
3. **Topic clusters** — pillar pages + supporting posts with internal links.
4. **Formats** — article, guide, video, template, tool — matched to intent.
5. **Cadence** — sustainable schedule; quality over volume.
6. **Metrics** — rank, traffic, engagement, assisted conversions; review monthly.

## Rules
- Every piece must be useful on its own (no thin content for ranking).
- Content must be truthful about the product — no overselling.
- Reuse research: one good insight can seed several formats.

# Social Content

## Purpose
Create social content that earns attention by being useful and specific —
not by shouting.

## Formats that work
- **Teach something** — one actionable insight per post ("How we cut deploy
  time 70%: 3 changes").
- **Show the work** — real progress, real numbers, real failures.
- **Answer questions** — from communities/customer support, publicly.
- **Point of view** — a specific stance on a real debate in the space.

## Per platform
- X/LinkedIn: text-first, one idea, links after the point.
- LinkedIn: professional outcomes + lessons; long-form posts for depth.
- Short video: hook in 2s, one tip, captions on.

## Rules
- No engagement bait, no fake screenshots, no inflated numbers.
- Link to real content; cite real results.
- Schedule sustainably; conversation > broadcast.

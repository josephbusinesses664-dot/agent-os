# Audience Research

## Purpose
Know the audience well enough to speak their language and reach them where
they are.

## What to gather
1. **Where they are** — communities, forums, newsletters, events, platforms
   (from searches + browsing behavior).
2. **What they say** — verbatim phrases, questions, complaints (community
   intelligence feeds this).
3. **How they decide** — what they compare, whose advice they trust, objections.
4. **Segments** — group by pain/job (not demographics); name each segment's
   buying triggers.
5. **Influencers & sources** — who shapes opinion; which are credible.

## Output
Audience brief: segments with language, channels ranked by where the audience
actually is (with evidence), decision journey, and top questions to answer in
content.

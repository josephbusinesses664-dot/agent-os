# Copywriting

## Purpose
Write copy that is specific, human and truthful — copy must reflect what the
product actually does.

## Principles
1. **Specificity beats superlatives** — "launches in 4 minutes" beats "fast";
   "used by 2,000 teams" beats "trusted worldwide".
2. **Benefit first** — lead with the outcome, then the mechanism.
3. **Customer language** — use the words users use (from research), not jargon.
4. **One message per paragraph** — scannable; short sentences; active voice.
5. **CTA clarity** — buttons say what happens next ("Start free trial", not "Submit").
6. **Honesty** — never claim capabilities the product lacks; never invent
   testimonials or metrics.

## Structure
- Hero: who it's for + the outcome + proof + CTA.
- Body: the mechanism (how it delivers), social proof (real), objections handled.
- Close: risk reversal, CTA repeated.

## Revision pass
- Cut 30% of the words; re-read aloud; remove every adjective that doesn't
  carry information.

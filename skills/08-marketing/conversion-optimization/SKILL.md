# Conversion Optimization

## Purpose
Raise conversion rates through measurement and experiments — never by
dark patterns or fake urgency.

## Process
1. **Funnel map** — every step from first touch to paid; find where drop-off
   is largest vs. industry expectation.
2. **Diagnose** — page speed, message mismatch (traffic source vs. page copy),
   friction (form length, required signup), trust gaps (missing proof), mobile
   issues, confusing CTA.
3. **Hypothesize** — "If we <change>, then <metric> because <reason>."
4. **Experiment** — one variable at a time, adequate sample, pre-registered
   success criteria; record results honestly (including failures).
5. **Scale winners** — roll out, watch for side effects on other funnels.

## Legitimate levers
- Speed (LCP < 2s), message clarity, trust proof, reduced friction,
  price anchoring, honest urgency, post-purchase upsell.

## Rules
- No dark patterns: no fake countdowns, no hidden opt-outs, no bait-and-switch.
- Kill an experiment when the data says so — don't torture the stats.

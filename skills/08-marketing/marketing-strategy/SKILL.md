# Marketing Strategy

## Purpose
Decide where and how to win attention: the channels, the message, and the
metrics that prove it — grounded in the product and its stage.

## Method
1. **Product reality** — what it is, who it's for, the core job, current proof.
2. **Audience** — segments by pain and buying behavior; where they hang out.
3. **Channel selection** — pick 2-3 channels to master (SEO, communities,
   partnerships, paid, content) based on audience location and budget; ignore
   the rest until the first ones work.
4. **Messaging** — one message spine (see positioning skill) across channels.
5. **Funnel & metrics** — awareness → activation → revenue; pick 1 north-star
   metric per stage; instrument before launching.
6. **Budget** — spend where CAC < LTV with evidence; kill channels that don't
   pay within the test window.

## Rules
- Marketing must understand the product before writing anything — no generic
  claims.
- Report honest numbers; a failed channel is data, not a failure.

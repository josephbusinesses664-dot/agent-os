# Competitor Analysis

## Purpose
Know each competitor well enough to differentiate and to exploit their gaps.

## Per-competitor brief
1. **Offer** — what they sell, to whom, at what price (evidence from their site).
2. **Positioning** — their claimed differentiators and messaging.
3. **Strengths** — what they genuinely do well (be fair).
4. **Weaknesses & complaints** — user complaints from reviews/communities
   (community intelligence feeds this).
5. **Moves** — recent launches, pricing changes, hiring signals.
6. **Gaps** — needs they don't serve; segments they ignore.
7. **Threat level** — direct/indirect/aspirational with rationale.

- Update per competitor on a schedule, not once.

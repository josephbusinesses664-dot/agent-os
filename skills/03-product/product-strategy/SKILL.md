# Product Strategy

## Purpose
Define what the product is for, who it serves, and what it deliberately
refuses to be — the filter every feature decision passes through.

## Components
1. **Vision** — the change the product creates for users (not a feature list).
2. **Target user** — who first, who later.
3. **Core job** — the single job the product must nail.
4. **Scope discipline** — explicit non-goals and what "no" protects.
5. **Outcome metrics** — the 2-3 numbers that prove the strategy works.
6. **Sequencing logic** — why the next milestone is next.

## Rules
- Strategy must make decisions *easier*: if it never changes an argument, it's decoration.
- Every feature proposal is checked against the core job; features that serve
  no metric or non-goal are cut or parked.
- Record strategy decisions in the project decision log with alternatives.

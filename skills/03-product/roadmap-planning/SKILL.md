# Roadmap Planning

## Purpose
Convert strategy and priorities into a sequenced plan: themes → milestones →
releases, with dependencies and decision points.

## Method
1. **Outcomes first** — the 2-3 outcomes the roadmap must deliver.
2. **Themes** — clusters of work that each advance an outcome.
3. **Milestones** — checkpoints with a definition of done (a demo-able state).
4. **Sequence** — order by dependency and risk (de-risk early); identify the
   critical path.
5. **Timebox** — rough windows, not dates, unless dates are contractual.
6. **Slack** — reserve capacity for discovery/tech debt.

## Rules
- A roadmap is a hypothesis: state what would change it (evidence triggers).
- No milestone without a definition of done.
- Distinguish committed vs. exploratory work.

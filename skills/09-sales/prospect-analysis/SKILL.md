# Prospect Analysis

## Purpose
Prepare a prospect so any outreach or call is specific, not generic.

## Brief format
```markdown
## Prospect: <company/contact>
- Business: what they do, size, stage, model (evidence)
- Situation: what's changing for them (hiring, funding, launches, struggles)
- Pain hypothesis: their likely problems (with evidence)
- Fit: x/5 — why (product-job match)
- Competing solutions: what they use today
- Angles: 2-3 personalized entry points tied to their reality
- Objections likely: price, switching cost, timing — with responses
- Next action: <one specific step>
```

## Rules
- Every claim about the prospect is sourced; nothing invented.
- Use their language from their own pages/posts in outreach.
- Personalization is evidence-based, not decoration.

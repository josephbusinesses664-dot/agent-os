# Sales Strategy

## Purpose
Design how the product is sold: who to target first, with what motion, at
what price, through which process.

## Components
1. **Segment prioritization** — segments by pain × ability to pay × reachability.
2. **Sales motion** — self-serve, transactional, or enterprise consultative
   (match product complexity and ACV); hybrid as it grows.
3. **Offer & packaging** — from pricing skill; entry price that lets deals start.
4. **Playbooks** — qualification criteria (BANT-lite), objection handling,
   discovery questions per segment.
5. **Pipeline** — stages with definitions of done; conversion targets per stage.

## Rules
- Qualify early and honestly; a won deal that churns in 30 days isn't a win.
- Sales content must not overpromise the product (handoff to support/retention).
- Record the sales strategy decisions in the decision log.
